# SAIGE Community Development Guidelines

Welcome to **Earthlings Connect – SAIGE Community Development**. This living document outlines our shared commitment to compassionate collaboration, responsible knowledge sharing, and sustainable innovation. It offers an empathetic approach for addressing misinformation, establishes community guidelines, and provides a roadmap for contributing to SAIGE’s open-source ecosystem and its automation practices.

## 1. Empathy and Compassion for Misunderstandings

1. **Assume positive intent.** Begin every interaction by recognizing that most people want to learn and grow.
2. **Listen to understand.** Practice active listening before responding. Invite clarification questions and restate what you heard.
3. **Acknowledge feelings.** Validate emotions first (e.g., “I hear your concern”) before offering corrections.
4. **Share context gently.** Offer evidence-based information with sources and avoid shaming language.
5. **Encourage reflection.** Pose open-ended questions that help individuals explore and evaluate their views.
6. **Promote co-learning.** Frame corrections as collaborative discovery (“Let’s look into this together”).
7. **Offer pathways forward.** Suggest trusted resources, mentors, or community channels for continued learning.
8. **Follow up.** Revisit the conversation later to ensure understanding and maintain trust.

## 2. Earthlings Connect Community Guidelines

1. **Respect every earthling.** Honor diverse backgrounds, cultures, and lived experiences.
2. **Center collective wellbeing.** Prioritize psychological safety, accessibility, and restorative practices.
3. **Communicate clearly.** Use inclusive language, cite sources, and disclose uncertainties.
4. **Safeguard data and privacy.** Adhere to privacy best practices, anonymize sensitive data, and follow applicable laws.
5. **Act with sustainability.** Choose environmentally friendly solutions and minimize resource waste.
6. **Collaborate transparently.** Document decisions, share updates openly, and attribute contributions accurately.
7. **Challenge ideas, not people.** Critique proposals respectfully and offer actionable feedback.
8. **Stay curious.** Embrace experimentation, invite questions, and celebrate learning moments.

## 3. Open-Source Guidelines & SAIGE Development Opportunities

- **Modular Architecture:** Develop reusable components (data pipelines, AI services, UX modules) to encourage interoperability.
- **Ethical AI Practices:** Embed fairness, accountability, transparency, and explainability (FATE) into every feature.
- **Sustainability Metrics:** Track social impact, environmental footprint, and community engagement indicators.
- **Documentation First:** Maintain architecture diagrams, API references, and onboarding guides.
- **Inclusive Roadmapping:** Host quarterly open planning sessions to surface needs from grassroots organizations.
- **Skill-Building Tracks:** Offer mentorship circles in data science, civic tech, community organizing, and regenerative design.
- **Global Accessibility:** Provide multilingual support and localization pathways.
- **Innovation Labs:** Encourage pilot projects (e.g., climate resilience dashboards, participatory budgeting tools) that align with SAIGE’s mission.

## 4. How to Contribute

1. **Explore the roadmap.** Review open issues, discussion threads, and quarterly goals.
2. **Propose ideas.** Use the proposal template to outline problem statements, solutions, risks, and impact.
3. **Create or claim tasks.** Coordinate on the issue tracker to prevent duplicate efforts.
4. **Follow coding standards.** Adhere to linters, testing requirements, and documentation norms outlined in CONTRIBUTING.md (coming soon).
5. **Document your work.** Update README sections, tutorials, or changelogs as part of every contribution.
6. **Submit a pull request.** Provide context, testing evidence, and screenshots or demos when relevant.
7. **Participate in reviews.** Offer constructive feedback and recognize contributors’ efforts.
8. **Celebrate releases.** Join community calls to highlight lessons learned and next steps.

## 5. Automation & Continuous Updates

- **Continuous Integration (CI):** Configure automated linting, testing, and security scans for every pull request.
- **Continuous Deployment (CD):** Utilize staging environments with feature flags to roll out updates safely.
- **Dependency Management:** Schedule automated dependency checks (e.g., Dependabot, Renovate) and review weekly.
- **Documentation Pipelines:** Automate documentation builds (e.g., MkDocs, Docusaurus) and publish on merge.
- **Community Dashboards:** Maintain real-time dashboards tracking issue activity, contributor stats, and community health metrics.
- **Change Logs & Release Notes:** Generate release notes automatically from merged PRs and share via community newsletter.
- **Feedback Loops:** Use analytics and community surveys to trigger iteration cycles and track adoption metrics.

## 6. GEPA, Consciousness-First Novel Framework, Edge Computing & Sustainability Iteration Guidelines

- **GEPA integration:** Define the GEPA scope (governance, ethics, participation, accountability) and map it to each initiative’s requirements, decision gates, and success metrics.
- **Consciousness-first novel framework:** Document narrative principles that uplift empathy, shared meaning, and planetary stewardship; align storytelling practices with community wellness goals.
- **Edge computing alignment:** Prioritize local-first processing, low-latency community services, and resilience in connectivity-constrained regions; publish reference architectures for edge deployments.
- **Sustainability iteration checkpoints:** Require each milestone to include energy footprint estimates, lifecycle impact reviews, and measurable sustainability improvements before scaling.
- **Versioned guidance:** Maintain a changelog of these frameworks, including rationales and community feedback, so updates remain transparent and auditable.
